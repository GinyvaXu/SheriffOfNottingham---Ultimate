# v1.6.6 - Four new rule mods

**English / 中文**

## English
- **New: Wild Card mod** — the host sets how many wild cards go into the deck before the game starts. Wild cards are legal goods; during inspection any wild card in your bag automatically counts as the goods you declared.
- **New: Sheriff Intel mod** — once per round the Sheriff may pay n coins (n = total cards left in the un-inspected bags) to learn how many contraband cards remain among the waiting merchants, as a range (0-2, 3-5, ...). Only usable when at least two merchants are still waiting.
- **New: Super Contraband mod** — each contraband type gets one super card worth triple its value and triple its fine.
- **New: Merchant Reputation v2** — legal goods that don't match your declared type no longer cost reputation; positive reputation makes you draw legal goods more often (reputation 5 = 90% legal per card); negative reputation raises contraband odds but is tuned down to leave room to recover.
- All bundled mod versions synced with the game build (1.6.6).

## 中文
- **新增：万能卡模组** — 房主在开局前设置牌库中的万能卡数量。万能卡属于合法品，查验时袋中的万能卡自动变成所申报的货物。
- **新增：警长情报模组** — 每回合警长有一次机会支付 n 元（n = 剩余未查商人袋中卡片总数），获知未查商人中共有多少张违禁品，但只能知道 0-2、3-5 等区间；仅当待查玩家不少于两人时可用。
- **新增：超级违禁品模组** — 每种违禁品增加一张超级违禁品，价值与罚金为该类违禁品的三倍。
- **新增：商人声望 v2 模组** — 合法货物与申报种类不符不再扣声望；声望为正时抽到合法品的概率梯度提升（声望 5 时每张牌 90% 概率为合法品）；声望为负时违禁品概率提高但整体下调，留有余地。
- 所有内置模组版本与游戏版本同步为 1.6.6。
